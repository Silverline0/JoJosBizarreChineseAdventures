Appendix F · Altitude Acclimatization Protocol — Shangri-La 3,300m

Shangri-La (3,300m) and Pudacuo (up to 4,100m) sit above the AMS (acute mountain sickness) threshold of ~2,500m. With a 3-day stay following an ascent from sea level, you are at moderate risk. The protocol below assumes healthy adults with no pre-existing cardiac/pulmonary conditions; VERIFY with personal physician 4 weeks before, especially the ophthalmology resident given the unique altitude–eye considerations (transient visual disturbance, possible retinal hemorrhage at >4,000m).

Day-by-day plan

DayAltitudeAction
Jun 18 (Lijiang)2,400mDay 1 mild adaptation. 3L water. Light dinner. Optional Diamox 125mg AM (start prophylaxis here)
Jun 19 (Lijiang)2,400mActive day OK. Maintain hydration. No alcohol.
Jun 20 (TLG → Shangri-La)2,400 → 3,300mSlow ascent over 6h. SpO₂ check on arrival; target ≥88% resting. Diamox 125mg AM & PM. Bed by 22:00.
Jun 21 (Shangri-La)3,300m"Climb high, sleep low" — Shika Snow Mountain summit 4,449m max (cable car, brief stay), sleep 3,300m. SpO₂ AM check.
Jun 22 (Pudacuo)peaks ~4,100mHighest day. Walk SLOWLY. Carry oxygen can. Snacks every 90 min. If headache >7/10, descend.
Jun 23 (Shangri-La buffer)3,300mAdapted by now. Lower-key day. Last Diamox dose AM.
Jun 24 (DIG flight)3,300m → sea levelRapid descent symptoms (uncommon): may feel sleepy or mild headache. Normalises in 24h.

Hydration target

4 L water/day at altitude. Carry a 1L reusable bottle each. Sip continuously; don't chug. Track urine: pale yellow = OK, dark = behind.

Symptoms — recognise these

SeveritySymptomsAction
Mild AMSHeadache, fatigue, mild nausea, poor sleepRest, hydrate, ibuprofen 400mg, Diamox if not already. Do NOT go higher.
Moderate AMSPersistent headache >6h despite meds, vomiting, ataxia, severe fatigueDescend 500m+ minimum. Oxygen if available. Telegraph hotel — Hilton Garden Inn front desk has 24h support; Shangri-La People's Hospital ~10 min drive.
HACE (cerebral edema)Confusion, hallucinations, severe ataxia, drowsinessEMERGENCY — descend immediately ≥1,000m. Call 120. This is life-threatening.
HAPE (pulmonary edema)Breathlessness at rest, frothy/pink sputum, blue lips, persistent coughEMERGENCY — descend, oxygen, dexamethasone if available. Call 120.

Oxygen sources in Shangri-La

  • Hilton Garden Inn Shangri-La: Request oxygen at front desk; they keep pillows for guests showing AMS symptoms. Or buy a portable can at hotel shop (~¥40).
  • Pharmacies (药店): Disposable 5L oxygen cans ¥35–50 each, every 200m on main streets.
  • Pudacuo park: Vendors sell cans at the visitor centre and at Shudu Lake stop.
  • DIG airport: Sells oxygen cans landside; available pre-flight for emergencies.

Things NOT to do

  • Alcohol-containing medications/mouthwash: avoid the same way at altitude — alcohol (even ethanol-based meds) impairs respiratory drive. Switch to alcohol-free mouthwash + check cough syrup labels before the trip.
  • Sleeping pills / antihistamines: avoid — same respiratory issue. Use melatonin only.
  • Hot showers immediately on arrival: drops blood pressure; wait 2h.
  • Heavy meals on Day 1: blood diverts to digestion not breathing. Eat small portions.
  • Pushing through severe symptoms: the descent works. Don't be heroic.

Appendix K · China Digital Arrival Card 入境卡

Required for all foreign nationals entering mainland China since 2024. Saudi visa-free entrants still need to complete this. Fill out online up to 72h before landing CAN, or at the airport kiosk on arrival (slower).

Quick reference

Official portalcard.nia.gov.cn (National Immigration Administration). Use only this URL — many scam sites mimic it.
When to fileWithin 72 hours BEFORE landing. Filing earlier returns an error.
CostFree. Any "service fee" page is a scam.
Time needed~10 min per person. Save the QR at the end as a screenshot AND PDF.
LanguageForm available in English; toggle in upper-right.

Step-by-step

  1. Open card.nia.gov.cn on phone (~Jun 10, after final ticket details locked).
  2. Toggle English (top right) and tap "Foreigner Arrival Card / 入境卡".
  3. Personal info: passport number (case-sensitive, no spaces), name in ENGLISH exactly as on passport, nationality 沙特阿拉伯 Saudi Arabia, date of birth.
  4. Travel info: Flight SV876, arrival date 2026-06-13, port of entry: Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN). Country last visited: Saudi Arabia.
  5. Purpose of visit: "Tourism" (sightseeing). Even though you have a watch fair, declaring "Business" triggers stricter review — only declare business if attending official Chinese contracted meeting.
  6. Length of stay: 12 days (mainland nights only, NOT counting HK). Your 30-day visa-free window starts 00:00 the day AFTER arrival.
  7. First-night address: Yangshuo Mountain Retreat / Alila Yangshuo. Include phone number; auto-suggest works for known hotels.
  8. Health declaration: answer truthfully. No recent fever / no visit to listed countries / no contact with infectious disease patients.
  9. Submit → wait ~5 seconds → receive QR code. Screenshot + save to phone wallet + email a backup to yourself.
  10. At immigration: show QR to officer on arrival. Takes 30 seconds.

Common pitfalls

  • Passport name mismatch: if your passport spells "MOHAMMED" but you typed "MOHAMED" — REJECT at counter. Triple-check.
  • Submitted too early: if you fill it > 72h before landing, the QR is invalid. Re-fill on Jun 10.
  • Multi-entry: One QR per entry. If you fly DIG → CAN on Jun 24 and come back to mainland from HK on Jun 27, you may need a new card for the HK→Futian crossing (verify with HK MTR Vibrant Express conductor).
  • HK arrival card: Hong Kong has its OWN separate arrival card system. Fill at gov.hk 72h before HK entry on Jun 25.
Verification tip: Print all 3 QRs (one per traveler) on paper as a backup in case phones die. Tuck them inside passport.
